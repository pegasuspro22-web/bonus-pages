NCLEX-PN – Full-Length Bonus Simulation 20

This online simulation of 100 questions is designed to reflect the style, difficulty, and priority-setting judgment of the NCLEX-PN, with all 8 content areas represented.

The simulation is organized into sections of 25 questions. The real exam is computer-adaptive and does not follow fixed sections — this structure is intentional here to help you keep focus over a long practice session and manage your pacing without reducing difficulty.

Complete the test in one sitting, without looking anything up, to obtain a realistic performance benchmark. When you submit, you will see your score by content area and a full review of every question with the correct answer and a complete explanation.
