Try one before you ask

This is question 1 of the Phlebotomy area, exactly as it appears in the High-Yield 100. Pick your answer, then check it.

A provider orders blood cultures, a PT/INR, a comprehensive metabolic panel in a gold-top SST, and a CBC. In what order should the CMA fill the tubes?

  1. Gold SST, light blue, lavender, blood cultures
  2. Blood cultures, light blue, gold SST, lavender
  3. Light blue, blood cultures, lavender, gold SST
  4. Lavender, gold SST, light blue, blood cultures
Show the answer

Correct: B. Sterile blood cultures always come first to avoid contamination, then the citrate (light blue) tube, then serum tubes such as the SST, then EDTA (lavender). Lavender too early would carry EDTA into the other tubes.

Exam trap: Putting the light blue tube first because 'coag tubes are sensitive'. Blood cultures still come before it.
