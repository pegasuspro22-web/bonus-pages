Question 4201

A patient missed two mammogram appointments and says transportation is the problem; what is the CMA’s BEST next step within scope?

Identify the barrier and offer appropriate community resources (e.g., local transportation assistance programs) and document the plan because simply “reminding harder” ignores the real obstacle.
Question 4202

During discharge teaching after a positive rapid strep test, the patient says, “I’ll stop the antibiotic when my throat feels better”; what is the CMA’s BEST response?

Reinforce completing the full course and review the dosing schedule using teach-back because stopping early is a common mistake that leads to treatment failure and resistance.
Question 4203

A patient is referred to cardiology and says, “They never called me, so I guess it’s not important”; what should the CMA do NEXT?

Question 4204

A patient with new hypertension is given written low-sodium diet instructions and nods silently while you room them; what should the CMA do FIRST to reduce the chance of nonadherence?

Ask the patient to use teach-back (e.g., “In your own words, what will you change this week?”) because confirming understanding beats “Do you understand?” which often leads to a yes without comprehension.
Question 4205
