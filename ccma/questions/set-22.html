Question 2200

During intake, a 58-year-old patient says they feel “a little dizzy,” and their vital signs show BP 86/54 mmHg with a weak rapid pulse while they are sitting upright on the exam table; what should the CMA do first?

Assist the patient to a supine position with legs elevated and call for the provider/activate clinic emergency response while rechecking vitals, because suspected hypotension requires immediate stabilization and escalation—do not continue routine intake or have the patient stand/walk, which can worsen syncope risk.
