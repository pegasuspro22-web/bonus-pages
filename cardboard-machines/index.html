Printable templates

One PDF for each project, in the same order as the book. The letters on every part match the Cutting Map in your book.

1Print at 100%

Choose "Actual size" in the print settings, on regular letter paper.

2Trace on cardboard

Cut out the paper part and trace around it, or glue it on with a glue stick.

3Cut on the lines

Dashed line = cut. Dotted line = fold. Grown-ups handle the craft knife.

1 in

Check before you cut. The first page of every PDF has a 1-inch test square. Measure it with a ruler: if it is exactly 1 inch, your parts are the right size.
