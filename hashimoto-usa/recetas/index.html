Veggie Wrap with Hummus

Serves4 servings
Prep15 min
CookNo cooking
DifficultyEasy
Why it helpsHummus provides plant protein, fiber and zinc, and raw vegetables add vitamin C and freshness. Avocado adds healthy fats. A quick lunch to take along. Choose gluten-free tortillas if you are sensitive.
Ingredients
  • 4 whole-wheat tortillas
  • ¾ cup hummus
  • 1 red bell pepper, cut into thin strips
  • 1 yellow bell pepper, cut into thin strips
  • 1 small cucumber, cut into thin slices
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 cup arugula
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • Salt and pepper to taste
Preparation
  1. Lay the whole-wheat tortillas flat on a clean work surface.
  2. Spread about ¼ cup hummus evenly on each tortilla.
  3. Distribute the bell pepper strips, cucumber slices, grated carrot, arugula, and avocado slices evenly over the tortillas.
  4. Drizzle with lemon juice and season with salt and pepper to taste.
  5. Roll the tortillas tightly and cut them in half before serving.
Nutritional value per serving
320Calories
40 gCarbs
10 gFiber
5 gSugar
8 gProtein
2 gSat. fat
10 gUnsat. fat
Tips & variations

For a spicier flavor, sprinkle 1 teaspoon chili flakes over the vegetables.

Spinach can be used instead of arugula.

Add ⅓ cup feta cheese for a creamier texture and richer flavor.

Quinoa Salad in a Jar

Serves2 servings
Prep15 min
CookNo cooking
DifficultyEasy
Why it helpsQuinoa and chickpeas combine complete protein, iron and fiber. Raw vegetables add vitamins and antioxidants. A gluten-free lunch that keeps well in the refrigerator.
Ingredients
  • ½ cup quinoa
  • ¾ cup water
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 small zucchini, cut into small cubes
  • 1 small carrot, grated
  • ½ red bell pepper, cut into small cubes
  • ⅓ cup cooked chickpeas
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of ½ lemon
  • 1 tsp honey
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
Preparation
  1. Rinse the quinoa in a fine sieve under cold water. In a small saucepan bring the quinoa, water, and a pinch of salt to a boil. Reduce the heat and simmer covered for about 15 minutes until the water is fully absorbed. Remove from the heat and let it cool.
  2. Meanwhile place the zucchini, carrot, bell pepper, and chickpeas in a large bowl.
  3. In a small bowl mix the olive oil, lemon juice, honey, salt, and pepper until well combined.
  4. Add the cooled quinoa to the vegetables in the bowl. Pour the dressing over the mixture and stir well.
  5. Divide the salad evenly into two jars and sprinkle with fresh parsley before serving.
Nutritional value per serving
320Calories
45 gCarbs
8 gFiber
7 gSugar
10 gProtein
1 gSat. fat
5 gUnsat. fat
Tips & variations

For an additional flavor note, add 1 teaspoon ground cumin to the dressing.

Black beans can be used instead of chickpeas.

Buckwheat Sandwich with Avocado

Serves2 servings
Prep10 min
CookNo cooking
DifficultyEasy
Why it helpsBuckwheat bread is a wheat-free option with magnesium and fiber. Avocado and sunflower seeds provide healthy fats and vitamin E, and spinach adds iron. Check that the bread is certified gluten-free.
Ingredients
  • 4 slices buckwheat bread
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1 pinch pepper
  • 1 small tomato, sliced
  • 1 handful fresh spinach
  • ¼ cup cottage cheese
  • 1 tbsp sunflower seeds
Preparation
  1. Cut the avocado in half, remove the pit, and place the flesh in a bowl. Mash it with a fork and add lemon juice, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
  2. Lightly toast the buckwheat bread slices until crisp.
  3. Spread the avocado mixture evenly on two slices of bread.
  4. Place the tomato slices and fresh spinach on top of the avocado spread.
  5. Spread the cottage cheese evenly on the remaining two slices of bread.
  6. Sprinkle the sunflower seeds over the cottage cheese.
  7. Close the sandwiches by placing the slices together.
Nutritional value per serving
350Calories
40 gCarbs
10 gFiber
3 gSugar
12 gProtein
3 gSat. fat
15 gUnsat. fat
Tips & variations

For a spicier flavor, add a pinch of chili flakes to the avocado mixture.

Goat cheese can be used instead of cottage cheese.

Add a few slices of smoked salmon for a higher-protein variation.

Millet Salad with Tomatoes and Basil

Serves2 servings
Prep15 min
Cook10 min
DifficultyEasy
Why it helpsMillet is gluten-free and provides magnesium. Tomatoes add lycopene, an antioxidant, and pine nuts add healthy fats and zinc. A fresh salad that is easy to transport.
Ingredients
  • ¾ cup millet
  • 1 ¼ cups water
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1½ cups cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1 small red onion, finely chopped
  • 1 handful fresh basil, roughly chopped
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Juice of 1 lemon
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp toasted pine nuts
Preparation
  1. Rinse the millet in a fine sieve under cold water. In a saucepan bring the millet, water, and a pinch of salt to a boil. Reduce the heat and simmer covered for about 10 minutes until the millet has absorbed the water. Remove from the heat and let it cool.
  2. Meanwhile place the cherry tomatoes, chopped onion, and basil in a large bowl.
  3. In a small bowl mix the olive oil, lemon juice, salt, and pepper until well combined.
  4. Add the cooled millet to the vegetables in the bowl. Pour the dressing over the mixture and toss well.
  5. Divide the millet salad evenly onto two plates and sprinkle with toasted pine nuts before serving.
Nutritional value per serving
320Calories
45 gCarbs
8 gFiber
6 gSugar
8 gProtein
1 gSat. fat
10 gUnsat. fat
Tips & variations

Add ⅓ cup feta cheese for a richer flavor and creamier texture.

Walnuts can be used instead of pine nuts.

Add 1 teaspoon dried oregano to give the salad a more Mediterranean flavor.

Zucchini Rolls with Cream Cheese

Serves4 servings
Prep15 min
Cook10 min
DifficultyEasy
Why it helpsZucchini rolls replace dough with vegetables: light, hydrating and gluten-free. Arugula adds antioxidants and cream cheese adds creaminess. A simple bite to share.
Ingredients
  • 2 large zucchini
  • ¾ cup cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp fresh herbs (such as chives or parsley), finely chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup arugula
Preparation
  1. Wash the zucchini and trim the ends. Using a vegetable slicer or a sharp knife slice the zucchini lengthwise into thin strips.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the zucchini slices for about 1–2 minutes on each side until lightly browned and flexible. Remove from the pan and let them cool.
  3. In a bowl mix the cream cheese with the chopped herbs, garlic, and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Spread a thin layer of the cream cheese mixture on each zucchini slice.
  5. Place a few arugula leaves at the lower end of each slice and gently roll them up.
  6. Arrange the zucchini rolls on a plate and serve.
Nutritional value per serving
120Calories
5 gCarbs
2 gFiber
3 gSugar
5 gProtein
3 gSat. fat
4 gUnsat. fat
Tips & variations

Spinach leaves can be used instead of arugula.

Add 2 oz smoked salmon for a richer and more complex flavor.

Sweet Potato Wedges with Herb Dip

Serves4 servings
Prep10 min
Cook25 min
DifficultyEasy
Why it helpsSweet potato provides beta-carotene, fiber and complex carbs, and Greek yogurt adds protein and probiotics. A gluten-free side, lighter than French fries.
Ingredients
  • 2 large sweet potatoes (about 1 lb total)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp paprika powder
  • 1 tsp dried thyme
  • 1 tsp salt
  • ½ tsp pepper
  • ¾ cup Greek yogurt
  • 1 tbsp fresh herbs (such as parsley or chives), finely chopped
  • 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
Preparation
  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Peel the sweet potatoes and cut them into evenly sized wedges.
  3. In a large bowl toss the sweet potato wedges with olive oil, paprika powder, thyme, salt, and pepper until evenly coated.
  4. Spread the seasoned wedges on the prepared baking sheet so they do not overlap.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for about 25 minutes until the wedges are golden brown and crispy. Turn them once halfway through the baking time.
  6. While the wedges are baking prepare the herb dip. In a small bowl mix the Greek yogurt with the chopped herbs, garlic, and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  7. Serve the finished sweet potato wedges with the herb dip.
Nutritional value per serving
210Calories
30 gCarbs
5 gFiber
7 gSugar
5 gProtein
2 gSat. fat
5 gUnsat. fat
Tips & variations

Quark can be used instead of Greek yogurt for the dip.

Add 1 teaspoon honey to the dip for a slightly sweeter flavor.
