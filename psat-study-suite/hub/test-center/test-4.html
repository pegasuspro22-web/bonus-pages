PSAT/NMSQT Test – Full-Length Exam Simulation 4

This online full-length simulation has been designed to closely reflect the difficulty, pacing, and overall testing experience of the official digital PSAT/NMSQT.

Unlike the practice simulations included in the printable Simulation Tests PDF, this is a separate 98-question exam, aligned with the format and demands of the real test.

This simulation is organized into sections of 30 questions. The official exam is not divided this way — this structure is intentional and helps you maintain focus over a long exam, manage pacing and time effectively, and reduce mental fatigue without reducing difficulty.

The test should be completed in one sitting, without external assistance, to obtain a realistic performance benchmark.

Your last full-length evaluation before exam day. Complete this simulation in one sitting to measure true readiness.
