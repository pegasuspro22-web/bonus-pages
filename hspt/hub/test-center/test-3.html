HSPT Test – Full-Length Exam Simulation 3

This online full-length simulation has been designed to closely reflect the difficulty, pacing, and overall testing experience of the official HSPT®.

Unlike the practice simulations included in the book, this online assessment is a full-length 298-question exam, aligned with the format and demands of the real test.

This simulation is organized into sections of 30 questions. The official exam is not divided into sections — this structure is intentional and helps you maintain focus over a long exam, manage pacing and time effectively, and reduce mental fatigue without reducing difficulty.

The test should be completed in one sitting, without external assistance, to obtain a realistic performance benchmark.

Challenge your analytical reasoning and build test endurance with timed practice designed to simulate real exam pressure.

  • Suggested time: 2.5 hours (150 minutes)
  • Try not to pause once started — if you must stop, your progress is saved
  • Treat this as test day

Your progress is saved automatically on this device. If you close the page or it reloads, open this test again and choose Resume.
