ParaPro Test – Full-Length Exam Simulation 1

This online full-length simulation has been designed to closely reflect the difficulty, pacing, and overall testing experience of the official ParaPro Assessment.

Unlike the practice simulations included in the book, this is a separate 90-question exam, aligned with the format and demands of the real test.

This simulation is organized into sections of 30 questions. The official exam is not divided into sections — this structure is intentional and helps you maintain focus over a long exam, manage pacing and time effectively, and reduce mental fatigue without reducing difficulty.

The test should be completed in one sitting, without external assistance, to obtain a realistic performance benchmark.

Take this first timed simulation to establish your performance baseline and identify priority areas before advancing further.

  • Suggested time: 90 minutes
  • Do not pause the test once started
  • Treat this as test day
