Core Counseling Attributes

Q86Reflecting in group
After her first meeting of a recovery group, a client says she felt relieved to hear others describe the same struggles, but her hands shook when she spoke. Which response best reflects her experience?

Why C is correct

The reflection captures both the relief and the difficulty without judging either.

Why the others are wrong

A. This question shifts away from her experience.

B. This advises her instead of reflecting what she said.

D. Telling her not to feel exposed dismisses her feeling.

Q87Cultural humility at intake
At an intake, a wife answers only after her husband looks at her, and he speaks for her twice. Which response best shows cultural humility and attention to comfort?

Why D is correct

Individual contact lets each partner speak freely while the counselor avoids assumptions about the couple's roles.

Why the others are wrong

A. Assuming and reinforcing roles is stereotyping.

B. This imposes the counselor's norms and may embarrass the couple.

C. Staying together ignores the chance to check comfort and safety.

Q88Multicultural humility
A Hmong client says that in her family, problems are not discussed outside the home. Which response best shows multicultural humility?

Why B is correct

The counselor reflects her values and invites her to define what would help.

Why the others are wrong

A. Placing the whole burden of education on her is inappropriate.

C. Generalizing from experience is stereotyping.

D. This challenges her values.

Q89Respect for identity
A transgender teenager says his parents keep using his former name even though he has asked them not to. Which response best shows respect and acceptance?

Why A is correct

The response validates his pain and invites him to explain his family without judgment.

Why the others are wrong

B. Questioning his sensitivity is invalidating.

C. This dismisses his experience.

D. Advising patience minimizes his feelings.

Q90Counselor self-awareness
A counselor notices a strong personal reaction when a client describes ending a pregnancy. What is the best action?

Why C is correct

Self-awareness, bracketing, and supervision protect the client from the counselor's values.

Why the others are wrong

A. Ignoring reactions can leak into the work.

B. A reaction alone does not require referral.

D. Sharing personal views imposes values on the client.

Q91Non-judgmental stance
A client from an Indian family says her parents expect her to accept an arranged marriage, and she is unsure. Which response best shows a non-judgmental stance?

Why D is correct

The counselor shows respect for her family values and invites her view.

Why the others are wrong

A. This implies a judgment about her culture.

B. Calling it normal minimizes her particular situation.

C. This takes a side and judges her family.

Q92Empathic responding to spiritual practice
A client mentions that his family held a healing ceremony for him before he started counseling. Which response best shows empathy and cultural humility?

Why D is correct

The counselor acknowledges the practice and asks about its meaning rather than assuming.

Why the others are wrong

A. Claiming to know prevents learning from the client.

B. This dismisses an important part of the client's life.

C. Deciding how to use it without asking assumes meaning.

Q93Values and what feels safe
A client raised in a rural family says he was taught to keep problems private and handle them alone. Which response best shows awareness of his values and of the counselor's impact?

Why A is correct

The counselor respects his values and asks how they affect the counseling relationship.

Why the others are wrong

B. This implies his values are a problem.

C. This reassures without exploring his values.

D. This pressures him and ignores his values.

Q94Conflict and attunement
A client says her sister commented on her weight at a family dinner. She felt hurt and did not respond, because she wants to keep the peace. Which response best reflects her?

Why C is correct

The reflection names both the feeling and the value behind her choice.

Why the others are wrong

A. This takes sides and adds an emotion she did not state.

B. This moves to action against her stated wish to protect the relationship.

D. This questions her perception.

Q95Genuineness
A client in recovery calls the counselor late at night after drinking and then comes to the next session. The counselor felt worried. Which response best shows genuineness?

Why B is correct

A congruent statement of care followed by curiosity keeps the focus on understanding.

Why the others are wrong

A. Expressing disappointment shames the client.

C. This sounds accusing.

D. This avoids the relapse and the counselor's own reaction.

Q96Unconditional positive regard
A client says her religion opposes divorce, yet she is thinking about leaving her marriage. Which response best shows unconditional positive regard?

Why A is correct

The counselor values both parts of her struggle and offers a space free of judgment.

Why the others are wrong

B. This takes sides against her faith.

C. This steers her with a value judgment.

D. Bringing in the counselor's beliefs damages the alliance.

Q97Gender identity in the family
A teenager says her mother keeps getting her pronouns wrong, but her mother is trying. Both want to stay close. Which response best shows sensitivity to gender identity?

Why C is correct

The reflection honors her identity and the relationship goal.

Why the others are wrong

A. This minimizes the teen's feelings.

B. This dismisses what matters to her.

D. This changes the subject away from her identity.

Q98Respect before joining a group
A client is reluctant to join a group because he would be the only Black man in it. Which response best shows cultural humility?

Why A is correct

The counselor takes the concern seriously and asks what would make joining workable.

Why the others are wrong

B. This pushes him without addressing the concern.

C. This minimizes his experience.

D. This dismisses a real concern.

Q99Empathic reflection
A woman facing a cancer treatment decision says she is terrified but also hopeful, and she does not want others to decide for her. Which response best reflects her?

Why C is correct

The reflection captures her mixed feelings and her wish for autonomy.

Why the others are wrong

A. This changes the focus to a skill she did not ask for.

B. General reassurance does not reflect what she said.

D. This undermines her autonomy.

Q100Noticing a personal assumption
A client from Nigeria says his family is praying for his recovery, and the counselor privately assumes he is avoiding treatment. What is the best action?

Why B is correct

Bracketing the assumption and asking about meaning keeps the counselor open and respectful.

Why the others are wrong

A. This pressures him and still rests on an unexamined assumption.

C. Avoiding the topic misses clinically relevant information.

D. Acting on the assumption without asking is judgmental.
