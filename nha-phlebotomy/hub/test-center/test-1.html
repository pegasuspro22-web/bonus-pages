NHA Phlebotomy Test – Full-Length Exam Simulation 1

This online full-length simulation has been designed to closely reflect the difficulty, pacing, and overall testing experience of the official NHA CPT exam.

Unlike the practice simulations included in the printable Simulation Tests PDF, this is a separate exam, aligned with the format and demands of the real test.

The test should be completed in one sitting, without external assistance, to obtain a realistic performance benchmark.

Take this first timed simulation to establish your performance baseline and identify priority areas before advancing further.
