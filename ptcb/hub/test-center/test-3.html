PTCB Test – Full-Length Exam Simulation 3

This online full-length simulation has been designed to closely reflect the difficulty, pacing, and overall testing experience of the official PTCE.

This simulation is a full-length 90-question exam, aligned with the format and demands of the real test — 80 scored, 10 unscored, presented together as on exam day.

The test is organized into 3 sections of 30 questions to help you maintain focus and manage pacing without reducing difficulty. The official exam is not divided into sections.

The test should be completed in one sitting, without external assistance, to obtain a realistic performance benchmark.

Challenge your judgment on scenario-based questions and build test endurance with timed practice designed to simulate real exam pressure.

  • Suggested time: 110 minutes
  • Do not pause the test once started
  • Treat this as test day
