Domain 3 of 5 · 17 questions

Elements of Interprofessional Care

Q48Speech-language pathology
A patient recovering from a stroke coughs after sips of water and has a wet voice. Which team member should evaluate next?

Why A is correct

SLPs assess swallowing function and recommend a safe diet, which reduces aspiration risk.

Why the others are wrong

B. Gait training does not address swallowing.

C. Oxygen titration will not make swallowing safe.

D. Housing is unrelated to the swallowing problem.

Q49Nutrition consult
A patient with a stage 3 pressure injury has eaten less than half of meals for a week and has lost weight. Which referral is most appropriate?

Why A is correct

Healing requires adequate protein and calories, which a dietitian can plan.

Why the others are wrong

B. OT does not manage nutrition.

C. Spiritual care does not address malnutrition.

D. A review alone does not treat inadequate intake.

Q50Occupational therapy
A patient with severe rheumatoid arthritis in the hands cannot button clothes or hold utensils. Which team member is best suited to help?

Why D is correct

OTs teach activities of daily living and supply adaptive equipment.

Why the others are wrong

A. Insurance does not address the functional deficit.

B. The need is hand function, not speech.

C. Breathing is not the problem.

Q51Pharmacist role
An older adult takes 14 medications, including two for the same condition, and has new dizziness. Which resource is most useful?

Why D is correct

Pharmacists identify duplications, interactions, and high-risk drugs in polypharmacy.

Why the others are wrong

A. Diet alone does not address duplicate drugs.

B. Balance training does not fix drug-related causes.

C. Emotional support does not review the regimen.

Q52Case management
A patient being discharged after a stroke needs a hospital bed, a wheelchair, and a home health aide, and is unsure about insurance coverage. Which team member coordinates this?

Why D is correct

Case managers coordinate resources and ensure a safe transition.

Why the others are wrong

A. Insurance authorization is outside occupational therapy.

B. Coverage is not a dietitian function.

C. Dietitians do not arrange home equipment.

Q53Respiratory therapy
A patient with cystic fibrosis needs teaching about an airway clearance vest and nebulized treatments. Which team member is best prepared to teach this?

Why C is correct

RTs are specialized in airway clearance devices and inhaled therapies.

Why the others are wrong

A. Social workers address psychosocial needs.

B. Dietitians focus on nutrition.

D. OTs address daily activities rather than airway devices.

Q54Ostomy nurse
A patient with a new colostomy has peristomal skin breakdown and recurrent pouch leaks. Which consultation is most appropriate?

Why C is correct

WOC nurses are specialists in pouching systems and peristomal skin care.

Why the others are wrong

A. The problem is not respiratory.

B. Speech therapy is unrelated.

D. Emotional support may help later, but the immediate need is a pouching solution.

Q55Chaplain
A patient facing surgery asks to speak with someone about religious rituals before the procedure. Which team member should the RN contact?

Why D is correct

Spiritual care staff address religious needs and support coping.

Why the others are wrong

A. PT focuses on mobility.

B. Pharmacists manage medications.

C. Case managers handle discharge resources.

Q56Social work
An older patient being discharged lives alone, has bruises of unclear origin, and says a caregiver controls money. Which referral is most appropriate?

Why A is correct

Social workers assess abuse, safety, and resources, and help meet reporting duties.

Why the others are wrong

B. Hand function is not the concern.

C. Gait is not the concern.

D. Nutrition does not address safety.

Q57Hospice eligibility
A patient with advanced cancer has stopped disease-directed treatment, and the provider estimates a prognosis of six months or less. Which service fits best?

Why B is correct

Hospice serves patients with a limited life expectancy who choose comfort-focused care.

Why the others are wrong

A. Rehabilitation aims to restore function and is not the match.

C. Therapy alone does not address comfort needs.

D. Rehab is not the goal of this patient.

Q58Home health
A patient who is homebound after surgery needs daily wound care and intravenous antibiotics. Which service is most appropriate?

Why C is correct

Skilled nursing is needed for ongoing wound care and infusions at home.

Why the others are wrong

A. Meals do not treat the wound.

B. PT does not provide wound care or infusions.

D. Volunteers cannot provide skilled nursing.

Q59Telehealth
A patient with well-controlled hypertension lives two hours from the clinic and has a smartphone. Which option is most appropriate for a routine follow-up?

Why A is correct

Telehealth improves access when the patient has the equipment and the condition is suitable.

Why the others are wrong

B. This ignores a practical, safe alternative.

C. A pamphlet cannot replace monitoring.

D. Routine monitoring should continue.

Q60Structured handoff
During a structured handoff using I-PASS, which information is given first?

Why C is correct

I-PASS begins with illness severity so the receiving nurse knows who needs closer attention.

Why the others are wrong

A. Actions come after the summary.

B. Receiver synthesis closes the handoff.

D. Contingency planning follows the patient summary.

Q61Late documentation entry
An RN forgot to chart a dressing change completed earlier in the shift. How should the RN document it?

Why A is correct

Late entries must be clearly identified so the record remains accurate and honest.

Why the others are wrong

B. Records must never be erased.

C. Omission leaves care undocumented.

D. Back-dating falsifies the record.

Q62Clinical decision support
An electronic alert warns that a prescribed antibiotic may interact with a patient's current anticoagulant. What should the RN do?

Why B is correct

Alerts are safety tools that need professional judgment, not automatic override.

Why the others are wrong

A. Review must occur before administration.

C. Passing the task along does not resolve the safety concern.

D. Overriding without review risks harm.

Q63Medication reconciliation
A patient is admitted from home with a bag of pill bottles and a handwritten list. When should the RN start medication reconciliation?

Why A is correct

Reconciliation at transitions prevents omissions, duplications, and interactions.

Why the others are wrong

B. Reconciliation is routine and should not rely on patient complaints.

C. Waiting until discharge misses early discrepancies.

D. Medications may be given incorrectly in the meantime.

Q64Palliative care
How does palliative care differ from hospice?

Why A is correct

Palliative care aims to relieve symptoms and stress and is not limited to the last months of life.

Why the others are wrong

B. Palliative care works with the primary team.

C. That describes hospice, not palliative care.

D. The six-month prognosis relates to hospice eligibility.
