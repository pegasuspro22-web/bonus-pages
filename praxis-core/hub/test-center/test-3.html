Praxis Core Test – Full-Length Exam Simulation 3

This online full-length simulation has been designed to closely reflect the difficulty, pacing, and overall testing experience of the official Praxis Core Assessment.

Unlike the practice simulations included in the book, this is a separate ~154-question exam covering all three sections (Reading, Writing, Mathematics), aligned with the format and demands of the real test. Selected-response questions only — the two essay tasks are not part of this online simulation.

This simulation is organized into checkpoints of 30 questions to help you pace yourself across all three sections in one sitting, manage pacing and time effectively, and reduce mental fatigue without reducing difficulty.

The test should be completed in one sitting, without external assistance, to obtain a realistic performance benchmark.

Challenge your analytical reasoning and build test endurance with timed practice designed to simulate real exam pressure.

  • Suggested time: 2 hours 30 minutes (150 minutes)
  • Do not pause the test once started
  • Treat this as test day
